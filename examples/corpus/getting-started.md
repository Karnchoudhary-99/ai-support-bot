# Getting Started

Welcome to Acme Cloud! This guide walks you through creating your account,
deploying your first project, and inviting your team.

## Create an account

Sign up at acme.example.com with your work email. You will receive a
verification link that expires after 24 hours. Click the link to activate
your workspace.

## Deploy your first project

From the dashboard, click **New Project**, pick a region closest to your
users, and connect your Git repository. Acme builds and deploys on every
push to the `main` branch. The first deployment usually takes under two
minutes.

## Invite your team

Go to **Settings → Members** and invite teammates by email. Members get the
Developer role by default; workspace owners can promote them to Admin.
