# Billing and Plans

Acme Cloud offers three plans: Starter (free), Pro ($20 per seat per month),
and Enterprise (custom pricing).

## How billing works

Billing is per seat per month, charged to the card on file on the first of
each month. You can add or remove seats at any time; changes are prorated
to the day.

## Upgrading and downgrading

Upgrade from **Settings → Billing → Change Plan**. Upgrades take effect
immediately. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing cycle,
and your data is kept for 30 days after a downgrade.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, we retry after 3 days and again after 7 days. Your
workspace enters read-only mode after the second failed attempt. Update your
payment method in **Settings → Billing** to restore full access. Contact
billing@acme.example.com for invoices or tax documents.
